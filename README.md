# Dombey Capital Research

Dombey Capital provides independent equity research for institutional investors, family offices and sophisticated allocators. The firm focuses on special situations and structural dislocations across global equities, combining fundamental analysis, forensic analysis and artificial intelligence with disciplined risk awareness.

Website: https://www.dombey.capital/
Research publication: https://dombeycap.substack.com/
LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/dombey/

Core resources:
- The Phoenix Report — weekly flagship special-situations research
- Daily Phoenix Screener — trading-morning situational awareness in DombeyAI
- Biweekly Catalyst Briefing — forthcoming corporate and market events
- Options Research — event-driven derivatives context
- Earnings Conviction Model — proprietary earnings research resource
- DombeyAI Portal — member environment for tools, models and the historical record

This repository is a public reference page for Dombey Capital research and links.
